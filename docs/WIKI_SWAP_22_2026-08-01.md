# The 22 wiki upgrades, swapped in - 10 approved, 12 queued (2026-08-01)

Operator-directed, on the evidence in `docs/WIKI_VS_FIRSTINITIAL_2026-08-01.md`:
of the 77 corpus images confirmed to be the same artwork as a canonical wiki
splash, 22 were a clear upgrade on BOTH axes (more native pixels AND sharper at
common scale, measured from the original bytes). Those 22 have been swapped -
the wiki original is now the `_firstinitial` and first pass has been re-run.

**Result: 22 processed, 0 FAIL, 0 HELD, and all 22 now approved into
`2.First Pass Done`** - 10 on a clean gate PASS, and the 12 FLAGGED ones on an
explicit operator override (`approve --force`, operator direction 2026-08-01,
after the flags below were reported).

Driver: `tools/lw_wiki_swap_oneoff.py` (+ 10 tests). Everything displaced was
MOVED, never deleted, into `data/wiki_swap_backup_20260801/`.

## The crop question did not have to be answered

The comparison doc flagged that all its numbers were on a 16:9 CENTRE crop and
that centre is an assumption, not a policy. That turned out not to block
anything: run through the pipeline's own `aspect_class`, the 22 wiki originals
are **10 `ok` and 12 `crop_ok`, zero `crop_heavy`**, with a maximum area loss of
**0.0504** against the shipped `AREA_LOSS_MAX` of 0.08. Every one is handled by
the aspect rule already in `lw_first_pass`, so no new crop policy was invented
for this swap. The open policy question in `first-pass-alpha-letterbox`
sub-shape A is untouched and still open.

## Two traps that would have made this a silent no-op

**1. `select_source` prefers a fetched DeviantArt fullview over the staged
`_firstinitial`** (`tools/lw_first_pass.py:210-215`). **9 of the 22 had one.**
Staging the wiki original alone would have left those 9 upscaling from the old
DeviantArt fetch while every log line reported success. Their
`data/recovery/fetched/<slug>/` folders were moved into the same backup. There
is no CLI flag to override source selection - moving the folder is the only
lever.

**2. `_firstinitial` keeps the SOURCE's extension, not `.png`.** An earlier
comparison run globbed a hard-coded `.png`, found nothing for all 58 staged
rows, and silently fell back to reading the 2560x1440 `_firstdone` OUTPUT as the
source. Caught only because a provenance count read 58 "no _firstinitial"
against a slug known to have one. Both this and the dimension check are now
pinned by `tests/test_lw_wiki_swap_oneoff.py`.

## What was done, in order

1. **Fetch and verify all 22 first, mutating nothing.** Each original decoded
   and its dimensions checked against the plan the measurement was built on; a
   network failure could not leave the corpus half-open. 22/22 verified, 7.8 MB
   median, largest `Vayne DragonmancerSkin HD` at 11084x6425.
2. Stage `1.First Pass Scratch/<slug>/` with the new initial plus a **copy** of
   the Done manifest, copied before any move.
3. Move `data/recovery/fetched/<slug>/` aside for the 9 that had one.
4. Move the stale `2.First Pass Done/<slug>/` into the backup.
5. `lw_first_pass.py --batch <slugs.txt>` - an explicit slugs file, never
   `--all-scratch`, which would have swept the 20 pre-existing WIP scratch slugs.
6. `lw_pipeline.py approve <slug>` for the 10 the gate passed cleanly.

Provenance recorded per slug in `data/wiki_swap_backup_20260801/swap_manifest.json`:
the sha256 of the bytes that actually arrived, the declared sha1 stored under
`declared_sha1_NOT_asserted` (P3 established no host serves bytes matching it),
old and new dimensions, aspect class, area loss, and where the displaced folders
went.

## Gate results

`processed=22 pass=10 flag=12 fail=0 held=0`. All 12 flags are soft, and they
fall into three groups:

| reason | n | range |
|---|---|---|
| `halo_pct > 0.05` | 7 | 0.0516 - 0.1019 |
| `band_delta > 0.05` | 7 | 0.0521 - 0.1369 |
| `lpips` in flag band | 1 | 0.1426 |

(groups overlap; three slugs carry two reasons.)

The halo flags are the known `usm-halo-calibration` item, not a defect of the
new sources. These 22 now genuinely RESAMPLE - a 6000-11084px source down to
2560x1440 - so the unsharp mask runs, unlike the 46 reference pictures which
were exact-target passthroughs and flagged nothing. The census already settled
that `halo_pct` is monotone in USM percent and reads as a strength dial rather
than a defect detector.

`lpips 0.1426` on `shan-hai-lillia` is the largest source change in the set
(held 1192x670 against a 6000x3375 original, 25x the pixels): lpips measures
perceptual distance from the source, and the source is legitimately a different
and much better file.

## Overridden and approved: 12

Approving a FLAGGED image requires `approve --force`, which records
`gate_check: override`. That is a judgement the ROADMAP reserves to the operator
("approval is operator-only by design"), so these 12 were first left in the
NEEDAUTH queue with their flags reported, and approved only on the operator's
explicit instruction afterwards:

`silver-fang-akali` · `1341679` · `wallpapersden-com-digital-art-diana` ·
`wallpapersden-com-winterblessed-diana` · `star-guardian-jinx` ·
`league-of-legends-shan-hai-lillia` · `lissandra-league-of-legends` ·
`snow-moon-morgana` · `wallpapersden-com-hd-sejuani` ·
`prestige-inkshadow-yasuo` · `spirit-blossom-yasuo` · `high-noon-hecarim`

The override is recorded honestly rather than looking like a clean approval -
read back from the manifests, the 22 `APPROVE_FIRST` transitions carry
`audit.approval.gate_check` = **`pass` x10, `override` x12**, each override
keeping its `FLAG` verdict and the exact reason strings. That distinction is the
property `_approval_record` exists to preserve
(`tools/lw_pipeline.py:971-993`); note the record is nested at
`transition.audit.approval`, NOT at `transition.audit`, and a probe reading the
outer level sees `MISSING` on every row and wrongly concludes it was never
written.

To reverse one: `lw_pipeline.py reject <slug>`, or move its folder back from
`data/wiki_swap_backup_20260801/done/` to abandon the swap entirely.

## Known bookkeeping residue

`lw_pipeline.py scan --verify` now reports **HASH_MISMATCH on 21 of the 22**
swapped slugs. This is expected and was predicted by memory
`project-reprocess-done-slug`: the manifest's INTAKE transition still records
the ORIGINAL intake hash, and the initial has legitimately been replaced. The
`_firstdone` files are correct; this is bookkeeping only. (`1341679` is the one
that does not report, its manifest carrying no comparable recorded hash.)

A plain `scan` reports **anomalies 0, needs_attention 0**. Re-measured after all
22 approvals: `--verify` reports **32 HASH_MISMATCH total, 21 on the swapped
slugs and 11 on slugs this operation never touched**
(`chengwei-pan-1`, `fantasy-design`, `fury-tempest-sona` and similar) and
pre-date it.
