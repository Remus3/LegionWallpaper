## 2. CALIBRATED 1-5 ACCEPTABILITY SCALE

Combined distribution (n=451): 5 = 206 (46 pct), 4 = 195 (43 pct), 3 = 44 (10 pct), 2 = 6 (1.3 pct), 1 = 0. Corpus B skews higher (only one 2) because the personal collection was already self-curated; corpus A's gate reviews define the operative floor.

- **5 - Aspirational (A: bo-chen-vayne / B: 100f.png / A: deathwarden-kindred tier):** official-splash-grade or indistinguishable fan work. Crisp focal face, coherent hero weapon (or a deliberate weapon-free composition like B: 77_cleanup / B: 113_cleanup), disciplined palette, strong silhouette, readable at desktop glance. TOLERATED at 5: background mush/DOF, crushed darks, minor finger softness on secondary hands, stylized proportions, even a watermark if composition is exceptional (A: dfzypou).
- **4 - Clearly in (B: 106_cleanup / B: 149_cleanup tier):** one notable weakness a viewer registers - rubbery/fused visible hand, over-upscale gradients, edge-to-edge busyness, banding/halo risk, cleanable watermark - but face + composition + color story still carry the frame.
- **3 - Barely-mid (A: stellastria-ahri / B: 32_cleanup tier):** heavy debt visible at a glance: centered watermark, obvious AI tells (fused grips, incoherent ornament, plastic skin sheen, melting junctions on NON-focal elements), or a stiff lifeless mannequin pose (B: 237_cleanup). Kept only because the focal face or mood is genuinely strong. MINIMUM lw-gen promotion bar.
- **2 - Floor (A: viego-the-king / B: 230_cleanup tier):** structural defect - composite seam/alien band, AI melt intruding on FOCAL features, watermark occluding the focal face, majority-dead frame. In corpus only as restoration input; lw-gen output at this level = reject.
- **1 - Outside corpus:** broken focal anatomy, illegible subject, gibberish-dominated frame. Zero corpus members at combined scale; auto-reject.

### Scoring heuristics (B-verified; none contradicted, two added)
(a) hidden/cropped hands are an ACCEPTED dodge, never a penalty (B confirms broadly: many 5s hide hands); (b) visible fused/waxy fingers cap a candidate at 3-4; (c) background softness is nearly free; (d) focal-face quality is the single highest-leverage axis; (e) melt or incoherence ON the focal subject is the 2/1 trigger - B extends this: a watermark ON the focal face is equivalent to melt (230_cleanup); (f) generated images must be watermark-free - corpus watermarks are pre-cleaning debt, not a style license; (g) NEW from B: pose energy matters - a technically clean but stiff mannequin pose drops to 3 (237_cleanup); lw-gen QA should score line-of-action, not just artifact absence; (h) NEW from B: the signature weapon is optional at 5 IF grade discipline + dynamism substitute (77_cleanup, 60_cleanup) - but when a weapon IS rendered it must be crisp and structurally coherent.

## 3. STYLE-BAND GUIDANCE FOR lw-gen (combined n=451)

Counts: **painterly-semireal 289 (64 pct)** - **anime-painterly-hybrid 107 (24 pct)** - photobash-cinematic 35 (8 pct) - **anime-flat 7 (1.6 pct)** - minimal-graphic 6 (1.3 pct) - 3d-render 5 (1.1 pct) - other 2. (19 cross-corpus dupes are double-counted; both notations agree on band in every checked case, so proportions are robust.)

**Anime-flat remains an edge case at combined scale.** It grows from 2/179 to 7/451 but stays at ~1.6 pct - the doubling of exemplars does NOT move it toward the distribution's mass. What B adds is nuance: the band's ceiling rises to 5 (B: 164_cleanup, K/DA Akali - iconic cel look, bold graphic shapes, reads at distance), and all seven members (A: p2402-kda-evelynn, A: spirit-blossom-ahri-mono, B: 150/151/164/210_cleanup, B: 252f) earn their seats the SAME way: deliberate poster/graphic-design intent - selective color, clean negative space, confident linework, often K/DA or esports-poster subject matter. Default anime rendering without that poster discipline still has zero corpus neighbors.

Recommendation (unchanged in direction, stronger in evidence): retune toward **anime-painterly-hybrid** (24 pct; nearest reachable band for an Animagine base: painted shading, rim/backlight, bloom, soft bokeh backgrounds, material speculars) with **painterly-semireal** (64 pct, where acceptability skews hardest to 5 - and where ALL nine Vayne 5s live) as the eventual target. If staying anime-flat, the recipe must explicitly produce poster-graphic compositions (K/DA-style bold shapes, selective color, clean fields) to match its seven anchors - and even executed perfectly it is playing in a 1.6 pct niche of the operator's demonstrated taste.
